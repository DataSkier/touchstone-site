1. The service

Dodecahedron is a Shopify app that lets you sell "mystery" products. Shoppers buy one product at one price, and the app assigns items from a pool of your products that you choose. It adjusts your inventory, creates and updates orders in your store (including internal shipping orders that your team or your third-party logistics provider ships, unless you choose hidden-only fulfillment), and reports on sales.

Changes. Features may change over time. We'll give at least 30 days' notice of any change that materially reduces what the app does or changes how it handles inventory, fulfillment or existing orders. Orders already in progress will be completed under the behavior in place when they were placed, or with a migration path we describe in the notice.

Automated actions and their limits. By configuring a mystery product and its pool, you authorize the app to take these actions in your store:

  • reserve items from the pool by adjusting inventory;
  • record its assignments;
  • tag and hold orders;
  • create, update and cancel internal shipping orders;
  • copy tracking to your customers' orders;
  • restock items when orders are cancelled or returned.

The app is designed so that a retried or interrupted action doesn't repeat. When it can't confirm the result of an action, it stops and asks you rather than guessing. That applies when it can't tell whether an inventory reservation or an internal shipping order went through. The order is flagged for your review in the app, and the app takes no further automatic action on that step until you decide. Decisions you make in a review (for example, "It didn't go through") are your instructions, and are recorded.
