Getting started

  1. Install Dodecahedron and answer one question: does your own team ship your orders, or a 3PL?
  2. Create a mystery product. Give it a name and price, choose whether shoppers pick an option such as size, and set the swap quantity: 1 for a mystery item, or more for a mystery bundle.
  3. Build its pool. Hand-pick products, or choose a collection or tag to make a smart pool that updates itself.
  4. Choose the location it ships from. The pool's stock must be at that location, or the mystery product shows as sold out.

Dodecahedron creates the mystery product in your store with SKUs starting MYS- and keeps its stock in step with the pool. Publish it to your storefront like any other product.

Choosing how you fulfill

Within seconds of checkout, Dodecahedron tags the shopper's order mystery-consolidated and puts it on hold. It then creates an internal order named like #1001-mystery, priced at $0, with every item in one box: the picked items plus anything else the shopper bought. Ship that order, and its tracking is copied back to the shopper's order, which notifies the shopper as usual. The shopper only ever sees the mystery product.

In-house (recommended)

Your team ships the -mystery order. Its packing slip lists the real items, and your accounting and inventory apps see exactly what shipped.

Third-party (a 3PL ships)

Your 3PL ships the -mystery order. It needs one import rule first: see Setting up your 3PL.

Hidden only (for very small shops)

No internal order. The shopper's order keeps only the mystery product, and you look up the item to pack in Dodecahedron. Choose this only if one person packs every order and you don't sync orders to accounting or inventory software: those apps would see a mystery product sold, but not which item shipped.

With in-house and third-party fulfillment:

  • Unpaid orders wait. The internal order is created only once the order is paid.
  • Fraud checks are respected. Orders that Shopify flags for review wait for you to approve them. High-risk orders are never sent.
  • Address changes carry over to the internal order until it ships.
  • Split shipments are mirrored parcel by parcel, each with its own tracking.
  • Order numbers skip. Each internal order takes the next order number, so the next shopper's order skips one (for example #1001, then #1003). Shoppers don't see the internal orders.

You can change how you fulfill at any time in Dodecahedron's setup page. Orders already placed ship the way they started.

Setting up your 3PL

Most 3PLs import every new Shopify order. Before your first mystery order, set up these rules in your 3PL's order import settings:

  1. Required: skip (don't import) orders tagged mystery-consolidated. Without this rule, your 3PL would receive both orders, and the shopper's other items would ship twice.
  2. Recommended: blacklist SKUs starting with MYS-. Mystery products aren't physical stock, so this stops your 3PL from flagging them as unknown or out of stock.
  3. International orders: set customs values for mystery shipments in your 3PL. The internal order is priced at $0, so customs forms built from it would declare nothing.

Not sure how? Ask your 3PL account manager for an "order import exclusion by tag" and a "SKU blacklist". Most 3PLs set these up in a few minutes.

Other apps that read your orders

The internal -mystery orders exist only to ship the box. The sale is already on the shopper's order, so keep the internal orders out of anything that counts sales:

  • Accounting and ERP connectors: exclude orders tagged mystery-shadow, or with the order source mystery-fulfillment, from sales sync.
  • Shopify Flow and staff alerts: skip the same orders, so "new order" emails and similar automations don't fire twice.
  • Shipping and label apps (ShipStation and similar): skip orders tagged mystery-consolidated. Those are the held originals; ship the -mystery order.

For developers and integrations, Dodecahedron also saves a machine-readable record on each mystery order (field mystery in the app's namespace) listing its internal order and the real items shipped, with SKUs and unit costs.

Cancellations and returns

Cancellations

Cancel the shopper's order in Shopify as usual. Dodecahedron cancels the internal order if it hasn't shipped and puts the picked items back in stock. If the box already left, the order is flagged so you can follow up.

Returns

Refund the shopper's order, not the -mystery order, and tick Restock.

  • Everything came back: Dodecahedron restocks the real items automatically.
  • Only part of a bundle came back: Shopify's refund doesn't say which items returned. The order shows "Return received" on the mystery product's page. Click Mark returned next to each item that's back on the shelf, using the SKUs on the returned items or your 3PL's return report. If nothing will go back in stock, for example because it arrived damaged, choose Dismiss.

How inventory works

  • The mystery product's stock is the number of complete mystery orders the pool can fill at its location. It updates after every mystery order and pool change, and whenever a pool item's stock changes in Shopify, for example after a regular sale.
  • Picked items are removed from stock the moment the order comes in. Your inventory history shows the adjustment with a reference to the order.
  • Internal -mystery orders don't change inventory. The items were already removed when the shopper ordered.
  • Restocks from cancellations and returns are recorded with reason "Restock" and a reference to the shopper's order.

Reports and reconciliation

Sales report: Dodecahedron's home page shows mystery revenue, orders, units cleared and cost recovered over the last 60 days.

Reconciliation: lists every item shipped from a mystery order with its date, mystery SKU, share of the revenue, SKU shipped, unit cost, internal order, tracking and status. Unit costs are captured at the time of sale. Download it as CSV for your books.

Shopify gives apps the last 60 days of orders, so reports cover that window.
