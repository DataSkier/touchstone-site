Questions merchants ask

What is Dodecahedron?

Dodecahedron is a Shopify app that turns slow-moving inventory into mystery products. Shoppers buy the mystery product, and your team or 3PL ships the real items in one sealed box.

How can I move dead stock on my Shopify store?

Bundle it. Mix slow movers with fan favorites in a mystery product to increase perceived value, then feature it across your store. You recover value on aged inventory, and shoppers get a deal that feels like a gift.

How does a mystery product work?

You choose the pool it draws from: products you hand-pick, or on Pro, everything in a collection or tag, kept in sync. Each order gets one item or a bundle of several from that pool, and you can put a chosen product in every bundle.

Does the shopper see what's inside?

No. Shoppers see only the mystery product at checkout, on their order pages and in order emails. Dodecahedron never edits the shopper's order, so the contents stay sealed until they open the box.

Does Dodecahedron work with my 3PL?

Yes, and you don't need one. Your team or 3PL receives an internal order listing the real items, packs everything in one box, and the tracking number is copied back to the shopper's order. A 3PL needs a couple of simple import rules, covered in the setup guide.

What happens when an order is cancelled or returned?

The real items go back into stock, not the mystery product, so your inventory counts stay accurate. Orders cancelled before they ship restock right away, and returns restock when the box is received. If your 3PL handles returns, its own stock sync takes care of it.

How do I know what shipped in each box?

A reconciliation report links every mystery box to the items and costs that shipped in it.

What data does it use?

Your products, inventory and mystery orders. It copies the shopper's shipping name, address and phone number onto the internal shipping order in your store. It never stores shoppers' names, addresses, phone numbers, emails or payment details. See our privacy policy.

How much does it cost?

There's a Free plan with mystery items and bundles, up to 25 mystery orders a month. Pro is $29 a month with 100 mystery orders included, then $0.50 per order, billed through Shopify. See pricing.
