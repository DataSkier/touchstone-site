Dodecahedron

Clear aged inventory on your own store with mystery products. One product, one price you set. Shoppers choose what matters, like size or fit, and receive a matching item from your pool of slow movers.

How it works

  1. Build a pool

    Hand-pick aged products, or point Dodecahedron at a collection or tag and it keeps the pool up to date for you.

  2. Set one price

    Dodecahedron creates the mystery product in your store, with a choice of size or fit if you want one, and keeps its stock in step with the pool.

  3. Shoppers buy the mystery

    The moment an order comes in, Dodecahedron picks a matching item from the pool and reserves it. The shopper's order never shows what they'll get.

  4. You ship the surprise

    Your team sees exactly what to pack, or your 3PL receives everything in one box, with tracking copied back to the shopper's order.

Questions

Does the shopper see which item they'll get?

No. Dodecahedron never edits the shopper's order. The item it picked appears only in the app, and, if you use a 3PL, on an internal order that ships the box.

Do I need a 3PL?

No. Dodecahedron works whether you pack orders yourself or use a third-party warehouse. A 3PL needs a couple of simple import rules, covered in the setup guide.

What data does it use?

Your products, inventory and mystery orders. If you use a 3PL, it copies the shopper's shipping name and address onto the internal shipping order in your store. It never stores shoppers' names, addresses, emails or payment details. See our privacy policy.

How much does it cost?

Dodecahedron will be a monthly subscription, billed through Shopify. Pricing will be announced at launch.
