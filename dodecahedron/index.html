Questions

Does the shopper see which item they'll get?

No. Dodecahedron never edits the shopper's order. The item it picked appears only in the app and on an internal order that ships the box, which is never linked to the shopper.

Do I need a 3PL?

No. Dodecahedron works whether you pack orders yourself or use a third-party warehouse. A 3PL needs a couple of simple import rules, covered in the setup guide.

What data does it use?

Your products, inventory and mystery orders. It copies the shopper's shipping name, address and phone number onto the internal shipping order in your store. It never stores shoppers' names, addresses, phone numbers, emails or payment details. See our privacy policy.

How much does it cost?

There's a Free plan for mystery items (up to 25 mystery orders a month). Pro is $50 a month plus a capped 2% revenue share on mystery orders past the first $2,500 each month, billed through Shopify. See pricing.
