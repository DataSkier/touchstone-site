How Pro's revenue share works

  • What counts: the total of every order that includes a mystery product, item or bundle, after discounts and before tax and shipping. Regular products bought on the same order count too.
  • The first $2,500 of those orders in each billing cycle carries no revenue share. The 2% applies to the amount above it.
  • The cap: revenue-share charges stop at $5,000 in a billing cycle.
  • When an order counts: when it's placed and Dodecahedron assigns its items.
  • Cancellations: an order cancelled before anything ships is taken back off your usage.
  • Returns: refunds and returns don't reduce the revenue share.

The Free plan's monthly limit

Free covers 25 mystery orders each calendar month, counted in your store's time zone. Dodecahedron shows a reminder when 5 are left. After the 25th, your mystery products show as sold out until the 1st of next month, or until you upgrade. Orders already placed are always filled, and cancelled orders free up their spot.

Changing plans

  • Upgrading takes effect straight away. Mystery products that sold out at the Free limit come back on sale.
  • Moving from Pro to Free: mystery bundles show as sold out until you upgrade again. Nothing is deleted.
  • Plan changes and charges are handled by Shopify, under Shopify's billing terms.
