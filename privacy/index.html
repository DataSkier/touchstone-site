Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Touchstone Commerce LLC ("Touchstone", "we", "us") makes Dodecahedron, an app for Shopify merchants. This policy explains what data the app collects when a merchant installs it, how we use that data, and the choices merchants and their customers have. It also covers this website.

Our role

When a merchant installs Dodecahedron, the merchant controls their store's data, including their customers' data. We process that data only on the merchant's behalf, to provide the app's features, and following the merchant's instructions and Shopify's terms. Shoppers with questions about their own data should contact the store they bought from first; we will help the merchant respond.

What we collect from a merchant's store

Dodecahedron reads data from the merchant's store through Shopify's API, using only the permissions the merchant approves at installation.

We do not collect or store shoppers' email addresses, payment details or browsing activity, and we do not store shoppers' names, addresses or phone numbers. Dodecahedron does not place cookies or tracking code on the merchant's storefront.

Data we write to the merchant's store

To do its job, Dodecahedron writes to the merchant's store: it adjusts inventory, creates and updates the mystery product, creates internal shipping orders, adds tags and staff-only notes to orders, and saves app records on orders that list the items picked. This data lives in the merchant's Shopify store and is governed by Shopify's privacy policy and the merchant's own.

How we use data

  • To provide, maintain and support the app's features.
  • To troubleshoot problems the merchant reports, and to keep the app secure.
  • To bill for the app through Shopify's billing system.

We use data only for these purposes. We do not sell data, share it for advertising, or use it to build profiles of shoppers.

Artificial intelligence: Dodecahedron has no AI features. We never send shoppers' personal data to an AI service, and we don't use store or customer data to train AI models. To investigate a security incident, we may use AI tools, with training on our data turned off, to review code, error logs and minimized operational data such as order numbers and store domains. If any of this changes, we'll update this policy and tell merchants first.

Who we share data with

  • Shopify, which provides the platform the app runs on.
  • Infrastructure providers that host the app and its database for us, under agreements that require them to protect the data and use it only to provide their service.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it, or to protect the rights and safety of our users or others.
  • A successor, if Touchstone is merged or acquired, under the same protections described here.

A merchant's 3PL or warehouse receives the internal shipping orders through the merchant's own connection to Shopify, not from us.

How we process order notifications

Shopify notifies Dodecahedron when an order is placed or changes. Those notifications include customer details, but we don't store them. Our processing queue keeps only the order's ID. When the app processes the order, it fetches the specific fields it needs from Shopify at that moment. Our logs record order IDs, counts and error codes, never customer details.

  • When a shopper asks a store to erase their data, Shopify passes the request to us, and we delete the records we hold about that shopper's orders.
  • Backups: our database backups are encrypted and allow recovery for up to 7 days, so deleted data can remain in a backup for up to 7 days after deletion before it's gone for good.

How we protect data

  • Data is encrypted in transit and at rest.
  • Access to production systems and data is limited to the people who need it to run and support the app, using strong, unique passwords, and access is logged.
  • Test and production data are kept separate.
  • We keep backups encrypted, and we have a plan for responding to security incidents, including notifying affected merchants and Shopify as required.

Requests from merchants and shoppers

Merchants can ask us at any time what data we hold about their store, or ask us to delete it, by emailing support@touchstonecommerce.com. Shoppers' requests to access or erase their data reach us from Shopify through the merchant's store, and we respond to them as required. Depending on where you live, you may have rights under laws such as the GDPR or the California Consumer Privacy Act, and we will help merchants honor them.

This website

This website doesn't use cookies or analytics, and has no forms. It loads fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the page loads. If you email us, we use your message only to reply.

Children

Dodecahedron is a business tool for merchants and isn't directed to children.

Changes to this policy

We'll update this page if our practices change, and change the date at the top. For significant changes, we'll notify merchants who have the app installed.
