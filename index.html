Turn aged inventory back into cash.

Touchstone Commerce builds tools for Shopify merchants who have money sitting on the shelf. We help you clear slow-moving stock on your own store, at a price you set, without marking down your brand in public.

Our first app

Dodecahedron

Pool your aged products behind one mystery product with one price. Shoppers pick what matters to them, like size, and receive a matching item from the pool. Your stock moves, and no branded product is ever advertised below its price.

Why we exist

Markdowns cost more than margin

Public price cuts train shoppers to wait for sales, and they can put you at odds with brands' advertised-price policies.

Liquidation leaves money behind

Selling to off-price buyers is fast, but it usually recovers a fraction of what your own customers would pay.

Your store is the best outlet

Your own shoppers already trust you. The right offer lets them buy aged stock without you shouting about discounts.
